## What it is

A falling-sand simulation written from scratch in C++. Every cell on a 2D grid
holds one material: empty, sand, water or stone. Simple local rules create
emergent behaviour: sand piles up, water spreads sideways, stone stays put.

## Roadmap

- ✅ Console prototype: grid, update rules, animation
- 🚧 SFML renderer
- ⬜ More materials
- ⬜ Performance measurement: particle count vs frame time
- ⬜ Optimization
- ⬜ Shaders and polish
